# Limitations

- All operational data, sensor readings, scenarios, and model outputs are synthetic.
- No proprietary L’Oréal data, procedures, thresholds, costs, volumes, or system integrations are present.
- The optimizer is a transparent greedy heuristic, not a proof of globally optimal scheduling.
- A conductivity or turbidity signal cannot prove cleanliness. The UI prohibits automatic release.
- Water screening cannot establish reuse suitability; site quality, cross-contamination, regulatory, and human approval are required.
- No CO2e, ROI, payback, or production-capacity financial claims are calculated because plant-specific boundaries and costs are unavailable.
- Target integration architecture is illustrative, and not connected to L’Oréal systems.
