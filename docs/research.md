# Research framing

L’Oréal publicly reports a 2030 ambition to use 100% recycled or reused water for industrial purposes in its factories, and reports 56% for this metric in 2025. The proposed opportunity is therefore not a replacement for existing water circularity work. It is an **engineering hypothesis**: upstream planning and governed process signals may reduce avoidable modeled cleaning-water demand before it enters a circular water system.

The project intentionally makes no claim about L’Oréal’s proprietary sequencing, cleaning, sensor thresholds, reuse practice, factory data, cost base, or real performance. The public evidence is registered in `source_register.md`; all prototype calculations are classified in the interface and documentation.
