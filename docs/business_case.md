# Business case

**Not publicly verified — treated as an engineering assumption.** This prototype does not calculate ROI, payback, savings, capacity value, energy impact, chemical impact, or CO2e because the required site-specific prices, volume boundaries, implementation scope, and treatment methodology are unavailable.

For a pilot, the financial model must identify each input source and scenario range: metered avoided demand, water and treatment cost, chemical and energy use, labor / downtime boundary, instrumentation, software, integration, maintenance, validation, training, and the capacity-value methodology. Benefits must use the same common baseline as the impact ledger. Potential reuse cannot be counted as avoided demand without an approved, measured replacement path.
